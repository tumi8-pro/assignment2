Welcome to Lesotho

The Mountain Kingdom

Discover the Kingdom in the Sky

Lesotho is a small, mountainous country surrounded by South Africa. It is home to the Basotho people, a proud history and a welcoming culture. Explore highland scenery, visit historic places, and learn about traditions such as the Basotho blanket and mokorotlo hat.

From mountain parks and waterfalls to villages and scenic roads, there is plenty to discover. Start planning your visit on our destinations page.
